## 0. Core editor
- ✅ Rope, opens 100k lines in ~21 ms, atomic save, CRLF; Wayland/X11 window on CPU; scrolling with a short exponential glide (see "Mouse input and scrolling")
- ✅ TextMate highlighting (65 themes) **on a background thread** (the UI spends ~0.05 ms; immediate color on long jumps)
- ✅ Full editing: auto-indent, bracket pairs, JSX tags, multi-cursor, move/duplicate line, comment
- ✅ **Tree undo** (branches; command "View history branches")
- ✅ Tabs, quick open, restored session, reload when the file changes on disk
- ✅ Wayland and X11 clipboard, primary selection (middle click), IME (input-method text)
- ✅ **Terminal**: PTY + VT emulator, **5,000-line scrollback**, **alternate screen** (vim/less), application cursor, bracketed paste
- ✅ Zoom, horizontal scroll, huge lines, indent guides, bracket pair matching, color swatches, sticky scroll
- ✅ **Word wrap + folding**: `rowmap.rs` wired in through `docview.rs` (word wrapping, indentation folds, clickable gutter chevrons and "…", Ctrl+Shift+[ / ], commands Fold/Unfold region/all and Toggle word wrap; Up/Down/Home/End by visual line; 13 tests). Without wrapping/folds the map does not even exist (zero cost); with wrapping, 120k lines open in ~180 ms. Indentation folds + LSP `textDocument/foldingRange` (imports, comments, regions). Inlay hints now also show on wrapped rows (see "Inlay hints"); the minimap now draws visual rows, see "Possible follow-ups"
- ✅ **Minimap** (O(height) sampling, click/drag, toggled in settings; validated on real files)
- ✅ **Theme bold/italic/underline** in code + on-demand glyph fallback (+2 MB). Fixed: `github-dark-default` and `vesper` did not load (a new test loads every theme)
- ✅ **Fractional hi-DPI scaling** (1.25 / 1.5 / 1.75 / 2.0): `window.scale_factor()` is read on open and on `ScaleFactorChanged`; the whole layout stays in *logical* pixels (constants `TAB_H`, `ROW_H`, `SIDEBAR_W`… untouched) and `Canvas`/`TextCtx` convert to physical pixels when drawing (`ui::scale()`, `ui::sc()`): rectangles round at the edges (neighbors fit together), 1 px lines become physical hairlines, corners/rings/cards are antialiased at physical resolution, shadows, icons sampled directly at physical resolution, UI and code text rasterized at `size × scale` (code zoom multiplies on top; `cell_w` measured at physical size ÷ scale), minimap. Mouse and wheel (`PixelDelta`) are divided by the scale on input; `App::size` is logical; the softbuffer buffer has the physical size. Override: `ui_scale` in the modal (Appearance; 0 = automatic) and env `LUMEN_SCALE`; headless `--scale 1.5` (`--size` is logical, the PNG comes out `size × scale`). Scale 1.0 validated **byte-for-byte identical** (3 screenshots vs. the previous binary). RAM: the physical frame costs `w×h×4` B (1.5×: 1920×1200 ≈ 9 MB; 4K ≈ 33 MB) and is drawn straight into the softbuffer buffer (no extra copy; on Wayland wl_shm keeps up to 2 buffers) and paint CPU grows with area (2.0 = 4× pixels). Limitations: with fractional code zoom the physical line height rounds per line (±1 px unevenness); check marks and the dotted wavy underline are drawn in logical blocks (slightly jagged at non-integer scales); not tested on a real Wayland compositor (validated by headless screenshots)
- ✅ **Zed-style mouse input and scrolling** (`scroller.rs`, `doc_scroll.rs`, `app_input.rs`; ~35 new tests):
  - **`Scroller`** (shared helper, one standard for every scrollable surface, copied from the Keyboard shortcuts modal): wheel notches apply immediately and linearly (no glide, no acceleration, clamped, reversing never jumps), one notch = 3 rows x `scroll_speed`; touchpad pixels are exact and keep a short inertia when the fingers lift (wheel `Ended` phase, tau 320 ms); jumps (page, go to line, caret reveal) glide for ~80 ms (ease-out).
  - **Editor**: `Doc::wheel` (vertical in lines/pixels, horizontal in `Doc.left`/`left_target`); PageUp/PageDown and Ctrl+Home/End glide ~80 ms; dragging the bar/minimap and typing (cursor always visible) stay instant. **Horizontal scroll** with the horizontal wheel, touchpad X delta and **Shift+wheel**, limited to the widest line near the window (cached per version; without word wrap) and a **draggable horizontal bar** (`ui::HBar`, pair of `VBar`, 3 tests) at the bottom of the editor. Also works in the split panel (each panel animates its own).
  - **Sidebar**: same vertical logic (3-line step) and **horizontal scrolling** (`tree.hscroll`, content and icons shifted, thin bar at the bottom when a name exceeds the width); output panel with the same vertical dynamics.
  - **Drag selection** (`DragSel`, 10 tests): dragging after a single click extends (even after an existing selection), **double click + drag = by words**, **triple click/gutter + drag = by lines** (anchor = initial word/line, flips when passing it), **Shift+click** extends and continues with drag, a click **inside** a selection only collapses on release (dragging from there starts a new selection), **Alt+drag = block selection** (multi-cursor, one selection per line). Vertical and horizontal **autoscroll** with the pointer outside the text, speed proportional to distance (60 px/s + 10x the distance), continuous even with the mouse still (every frame). The primary selection is only published if not empty.
  - Headless: tokens `wheel:dx,dy[,x,y]` (notches, positive = down/right), `pwheel:` (pixels), `wheel-end`, `mods:shift+alt`, `down:x,y`, `move:x,y`, `up`, `frames:N`, `still` (renders until settled and prints the frame count) and `state`. Measured: 1 notch settles in ~13 frames (~210 ms), PageDown/Ctrl+End in 5 frames (80 ms).
  - **Lists and panes share the same `Scroller`** (`scrollreg.rs`: `Regions::anim`, one glide state per registered region): palette/pickers, TopMenu dropdowns, completion list, Git panel (changes + history), side-by-side diff, Markdown preview and the hover popup glide with the same acceleration, pixel-exact touchpad deltas (no truncation to whole rows), fling when the fingers lift and no overshoot. Row lists (pickers, menus, completion) keep the integer `scroll` of their owner and draw the fractional remainder as a pixel offset (rows shift up, one extra row at the bottom, everything clipped to the list); pixel lists (Git panel, preview) and the diff (fractional rows) write the exact position. Keyboard navigation stays instant: whenever the owner moves the list by itself (selection kept visible, jump, new list) the glide drops its state (`anim_sync`). Headless: `state` prints `Picker=3.68(frac 0.68)`, `Md=66.00`, `hover_scroll=…` per gliding region. Output panel/terminal/debug lists keep their own scrolling
  - Limitations: the maximum width considers ±800 lines around the window (a giant line far away only comes into range when scrolling there); the widest-line scan adds the hint cells of the lines whose hints are already known; inertia depends on the compositor sending `TouchPhase::Ended` (Wayland yes; X11 has no phase = no inertia); Alt+drag may be captured by the window manager
- ✅ **Split panels** (first version, two panes; extended to four panes with a 2x2 grid in "Possible follow-ups"): palette → "View: Split editor right" (`Ctrl+\`) opens a second, already focused panel with the same document; clicking another tab swaps the focused panel's document (two documents side by side). `Ctrl+1`/`Ctrl+2` or "View: Focus next pane" switch focus; clicking a panel focuses it; "View: Close split" keeps the focused panel's document. Each panel has its own scroll and cursor (even for the same file in both); 1 px draggable divider; the mouse wheel scrolls the panel under the pointer without changing focus; the focused panel's tab is underlined in the accent color and the other panel's in gray; the unfocused panel has no current-line band and a hidden cursor. Search, completion, hover and LSP follow the focused panel (`App::active` = the focused panel's document). State in `App::split: Option<Box<Split>>` (zero cost when closed), saved in the session since the follow-up below. The first version's limitations (shared folds/wrap, a single split level, no resize cursor, the reader and the diff closing the split) are lifted, see "Possible follow-ups"

## 2. TypeScript via LSP
- ✅ tsgo on demand with full project awareness by default; **lean mode** (≈800 → ≈220 MB) is an opt-in (`lsp_narrow`); project version or bundled. Disk changes/creates/deletes are forwarded as `didChangeWatchedFiles`, tsconfig/lockfile changes restart the server, open documents are re-diagnosed
- ✅ Diagnostics, hover, completion, definition, references, rename, format, organize imports, quick fixes, signature help
- ✅ **Rich hover** (`app_hover.rs` + `hover_md.rs`, ~30 tests): VS Code/Zed-style popup. Real Markdown (```ts blocks colored by the TextMate tokenizer with the current theme, `code`, **bold**, lists, quotes, separators, links, bare URLs become links); JSDoc in sections (`@param` with highlighted name + signature type + description, `Retorna`, `Lança`, `Exemplo`, `Veja também`, `@deprecated` as an "Obsoleto" banner, other tags); wrapping by width, maximum height with scrolling (mouse wheel) and a thin draggable bar (`ui::VBar`); never leaves the editor area (above or below the line, stable side while it grows); the mouse can **enter the popup** (scroll, click links/actions, drag to select lines, `Ctrl+C` copies) without flicker: the popup only closes ~280 ms after the pointer leaves both the symbol and the popup, or with `Esc`/a key/a click outside. Cache keyed by (document, version, symbol span): going back to the same symbol is instant with no new request. **Content**: for `interface`/`type`/`enum`/`class` tsgo only sends "interface User"; the app requests `textDocument/definition` and cuts the declaration out of the file (open buffer or disk, own lexer that ignores strings/comments/templates): full interface and type (a long union becomes one line per member), enum with implicit values (`Admin, // = 0`), class with only the public surface (signatures without body/initializer, constructor, `/** summary */`, "+N private members"), up to 40 lines with "… mais N linhas — ver definição"; function: signature + parameters + return + overloads (consecutive `export function f(...)`); variable/const: short literal value; a variable/property/parameter whose type is a project interface/class shows the expanded "Tipo X" (`typeDefinition`, ignores `lib.*.d.ts`); a function parameter with a named type in the same file (component props) likewise; import/`require`: module, resolved file (clickable) and `package@version` in `node_modules`; a literal (string/number/boolean) shows the type of the property/JSX attribute it belongs to (`key: "x"`, `prop="x"`). **Diagnostics** above the type (colored bar, code `TS2322` with a link to typescript.tv, action "Ações rápidas (Ctrl+.)"). **Footer** with discreet links: Go to definition, Go to type definition (variable/property/parameter), References, Copy type (the expanded declaration, when there is one). Nothing blocks the UI: the extra requests (`Pending::HoverExtra`) arrive later and the popup recomposes in place keeping its scroll. Headless: `hover:x,y;wait:ms;snap:name`, `wheel:x,y,n`, `down:`/`move:`/`up`, `esc`. **Declaration cutting is now tokenizer-aware** (`hover_md.rs`, ~20 new tests): the scanner skips comments, strings, template literals (nested `${ }` and templates inside them) and regex literals (decided from the previous token, like a JS lexer); interface/class/enum headers skip `<T extends { a: 1 } = {}>`, `extends mixin({ ... })` and arrow types inside generics; function headers stop at the real body (not at a `{` inside type parameters, return type literals or `x is { a: 1 }`); type aliases follow multi-line generics and conditional/arrow types; class members with regex initializers, `1 << 2`, comparisons or templates split correctly; decorators (`@Foo.bar<T>(…)`, `@(expr)`, object arguments) are skipped both inside classes and on the declaration line; overload detection ignores matches in comments/strings. Verified headless on a temp project: `.ts`, `.js`, `.jsx` hovers (including JS declarations reached from TS and JSX tags) with `lsp_narrow` on and off. **Text selection is per character** (`CharBox` geometry per line: code on the monospace grid, prose glyph by glyph; press = caret, drag = selection, `Ctrl+C` copies exactly the highlighted characters). **Italic is a real slant** (synthetic, `CacheKeyFlags::FAKE_ITALIC`) in hover docs, `@example` titles and Markdown emphasis; the old lighter-colour approximation only remains as a fallback when `TextCtx::italic_works()` finds that the slant does not render. Limitations: method overloads only when the signatures are consecutive; JSX text with a lone apostrophe can confuse the scanner until the end of that line; no double-click word selection in the popup; expanded type only if the declaration is in a file outside `lib.*.d.ts`; without tsgo (a file with no server) only diagnostics appear
- ✅ Expand selection, call hierarchy, **semantic tokens**, **code lens** (optional)
- ✅ **Inlay hints** (optional, `inlay_hints` in `config.json`/modal, palette "Alternar inlay hints"; separate switches `inlay_hints_params` (parameter names) and `inlay_hints_types` (types, return types, enum values), both on by default, applied to the server config and filtered on arrival): `textDocument/inlayHint` for the visible lines ±40 (500 ms after editing; edits shift the hints, see below), stored in `Doc.inlay`; drawn in gray/italic over a soft pill, in virtual columns (cursor, selection, clicks, brackets, diagnostics and sticky scroll are correct). Off = zero cost. Validated by screenshot with tsgo (variable/return/parameter types, argument names, enum, wrapped rows)
  - **Smaller font**: labels are drawn at 85% of the code size (`inlay::HINT_SCALE`, `TextCtx::draw_hint`, same baseline, italic, centred in their slot). Each hint has its own width in whole cells, `label_cells(n) = ceil(0.85 n)` plus one cell per padding (`Hint::width`), and every conversion (`vcol_in`, `hit_in`, `hint_cells`, `window_pieces_hinted`, `Doc::hit_at`/`pos_at`/`screen_pos`/`caret_geom`) uses it, so the grid stays exact with word wrap, folds, split panes and horizontal scroll (the text run carries blank cells where a hint goes; labels are drawn separately and clipped to the pane by pixels).
  - **Wrapped rows**: `row_hints` slices a line's hints per visual row (columns relative to the row start); caret, selection, clicks and painting use the slice.
  - **Clickable**: label parts with a `location` (tsgo sends them for types and parameter names) become links: Ctrl+click goes to the definition (pointer cursor while Ctrl is held over a link); a plain click only places the cursor. Double click on a hint applies its `textEdits` as one undo step (`Doc::apply_hint_edits`, `apply_char_edits`); tsgo sends no `textEdits` and rejects `inlayHint/resolve`, so for a `: T` type hint without edits the label itself is inserted at the hint position (the "add type annotation" edit); parameter-name and enum hints never edit.
  - **No flash**: hints follow edits (`InlayState::on_edits` from the recorded `EditRec`s: same-line text edits shift the hint columns, Enter moves the tail's hints to the new line, hints inside deleted text vanish) instead of being dropped until the server answers; the request pads ±40 lines and goes out at once (at most every 60 ms) when the viewport itself is uncovered, instead of after the 180 ms scroll debounce (a margin refresh keeps the debounce); known hints within ±400 lines stay until replaced.
  - Limitations: wrap breaks are computed on the text alone, so a wrapped row with hints can end up wider than the pane and its tail is clipped (wrapped text never scrolls sideways); the label width is rounded up to whole cells (average 0.5 cell of slack per hint, hidden by centring); block (Alt) selection and Up/Down goal columns still count text columns only; hovering a hint shows the hover of the token next to it; no `inlayHint/resolve` or hint tooltips

- ✅ **Big files are first-class** (`lsp_stream.rs`, `bigfile.rs`; ~25 tests, including bounded-memory assertions and one against a real tsgo): the LSP reader thread no longer builds a `serde_json::Value` out of whatever the server sends. Bodies up to 256 KB are parsed whole as before; bigger ones are **stream-parsed while they are read** with serde visitors chosen by the request's method (a policy registered per request id): `documentSymbol` is flattened straight into compact `Sym`s keeping only the first 3 levels (top level + 2) and at most 50k symbols; `semanticTokens/*` are packed as `u32` (at most 4M integers, delivered as `Msg::Tokens`); `foldingRange` keeps only the start/end lines (at most 50k ranges); any other response above 4 MB is skipped (the rest of the body is drained so the framing stays in sync) and an error response `{"data": {"tooLarge": true}}` is synthesized; oversized notifications and server requests are dropped. A test feeds a lazily generated 135 MB `documentSymbol` response and asserts a peak of < 40 MB on the reading thread (per-thread allocation counter), another skips a 50 MB generic response with < 1 MB. **Semantic tokens above 20k lines** (`bigfile::BIG_LINES`; the 20k cap is gone) use `textDocument/semanticTokens/range` for the viewport +/- 200 lines, requested again (120 ms debounce) when the view comes within 60 lines of the window's edge or after an edit, with a per-line cache bounded to 3000 lines (neighbouring windows are kept so scrolling back does not flicker; responses to an older document version are dropped); tsgo answers a window in ~10 ms. Files up to 20k lines keep `full`; `full/delta` is not used because tsgo only advertises `full` + `range`. **Outline / breadcrumbs / sticky scroll** work at any size from the trimmed outline; while it is missing (or does not cover the cursor) the breadcrumb chain is derived from indentation (`bigfile::indent_chain`: scans at most 20k lines upward, names `class`/`function`/methods/arrow consts, memoized per line and version). Limitations: the "Go to symbol" picker of a huge file only lists the first 3 levels; semantic windows are only kept for the focused pane's document; the outline of a huge file is rebuilt on every edit burst (one tsgo round trip)

CLI: `--install-user` (layout + launcher entry + icon), `--check-update`, `--update`. Verified locally end to end against a local HTTP server with throwaway keys; only the real GitHub release (secret, `generate-notes`, `releases/latest/download` redirect, signing in CI) can be verified in CI. - ✅ Markdown: incremental parse and layout, real images, footnotes, math, highlighting in code blocks, task checkboxes
  - **Incremental** (`mdview.rs`): the source is cut at blank lines into segments (fences, raw HTML blocks, lists and indented continuations stay together; merging is always safe), each parsed and laid out on its own and cached under the hash of its text (ops/hit boxes with y relative to the segment, so edits above only shift y). A keystroke re-parses and re-lays out one segment; the rest is placed by y offset and line mapping / hit-testing look into the owning segment (no per-keystroke copy of anchors). Measured (`cargo test --release typing_in -- --ignored --nocapture`): 2 MB / 142k lines / 47k segments, per keystroke including rope-to-string and a draw: median 11 ms; a 200 KB document ~2 ms; a width change re-lays out (cached per segment and width) but never re-parses.
  - **Images**: local relative paths only (`mdimg.rs`): png (`png`), jpg (`zune-jpeg`), gif (`gif`) and webp (`image-webp`), animated ones included (see below), svg (own renderer on `tiny-skia` + `roxmltree`: shapes, paths with arcs, transforms, viewBox, solid fills/strokes, `use`; a gradient paints its first stop). Sizes come from a header probe at layout time; pixels are decoded on a worker thread (max 2), scaled once to the drawn size, kept in a 40 MB LRU; the preview keeps redrawing while they load. `<img width/height>` is honored; remote URLs, `data:` and absolute paths keep the placeholder card.
  - **Footnotes** (`[^x]` / `[^x]: ...`): numbered by first reference as `[n]` links to the definition, definitions collected after a rule at the end of the document (`fn-<label>` anchors for `#fn-x` links); an undefined `[^x]` stays literal. Reference-style links (`[a][ref]` + `[ref]: url`) now resolve across segments too.
  - **Math**: `$...$` as a tinted monospace chip, `$$...$$` as a tinted block (centered monospace source, accent bar, "math" label); no LaTeX engine.
  - **Code blocks** are colored by the TextMate tokenizer with the current theme (shared tokenizers of the app; fence language by manifest id or alias: `ts`, `bash`, `rs`...), lazily when the block first comes into view and cached per theme (blocks over 800 lines stay plain).
  - **Task checkboxes**: a click on `[ ]` / `[x]` in the preview flips the source (one undo step, scroll kept).
  - Dependencies added (default features off): `png`, `zune-jpeg`, `gif`, `image-webp`, `tiny-skia`, `roxmltree`. Tests: ~25 new in `mdview.rs`/`mdimg.rs`/`app_md.rs`; validated headless with screenshots.
  - **Footnote back-links**: every definition ends (right edge of its first line) with a return arrow linking to `#fnref-<label>`, an anchor registered on the block that first mentions the footnote; an unreferenced definition gets the arrow but nothing to land on. References inside table cells have no anchor.
  - **Edited images**: each probed file keeps its mtime and size; the open preview checks them about once a second (`MdView::refresh_images`, driven by `App::md_tick`), and a changed file drops its cached size, pixels and queued decodes (a stale decode that finishes later is discarded) and the document is laid out again.
  - **Animated GIF / WebP** (`mdimg::decode_any`, `Anim`): the worker decodes every frame (GIF disposal methods composited, WebP by the decoder), scales each to the drawn size and keeps them in the same LRU budget. Delays follow browsers (0 to 10 ms mean 100 ms; nothing faster than 40 ms, so at most ~25 fps). Playing is timer-driven and only for what is on screen: `ImgStore::texture` is asked only for drawn images, it registers when the next frame is due and `App::md_tick` wakes the loop for exactly that moment (no continuous redraw; an animation scrolled out of view costs nothing). At most 4 animations play at once; an animation over 300 frames or 16 MB of scaled frames, or the fifth one on screen, shows its first frame with an "animado" badge instead. Validated headless (frames differ between snapshots; the badge case) and by tests with hand-built GIF and WebP files.
  - Limitations: animations always loop forever (the loop count is ignored); SVG text, CSS classes, filters, masks and real gradients are not rendered; math is not typeset; inline images in the middle of a line still end the paragraph text; `[^x]` definitions with several indented paragraphs follow pulldown-cmark's old syntax (extra paragraphs become code blocks)
